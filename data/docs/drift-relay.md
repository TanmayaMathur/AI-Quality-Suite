# Drift Relay compatibility

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

The Drift Relay joins 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi with WPA2-Personal. WPA3-only and captive portal networks are unsupported.

The relay is rated for indoor loads up to 8 A at 120 V AC. Motors and space heaters are excluded regardless of their current rating.

After a power interruption, Drift Relay defaults to off. The Restore Last State toggle is optional and ships disabled.

The relay has a physical override. Press it once for local switching while Wi-Fi is down.
