# Limited warranty and remedies

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

Harbor Bridge and Stillwater Valve Controller carry 24 months of coverage from the purchase date. Drift Relay, Aurora A19, and Morrow carry 12 months.

A warranty claim needs a dated purchase receipt and the unit serial number. A shipping label alone is not evidence of purchase.

Normal battery depletion, water damage to indoor products, and damage from an incompatible electrical load are excluded.

An approved replacement inherits the original end date or gets 90 days from replacement shipment, whichever is later.
