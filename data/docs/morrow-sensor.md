# Morrow contact sensor

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

The Morrow sensor takes one CR2450 cell. Install it with the plus face toward the lid.

Mount the magnet within 12 mm of the sensor when the door is closed. Align the two alignment marks before tightening the brackets.

A red flash every 30 seconds means the battery is low. Two red flashes immediately after opening indicate the sensor lost its bridge link.

Morrow is indoor-only. It can operate from 0 to 45 degrees Celsius in a dry location.
