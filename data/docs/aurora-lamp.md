# Aurora lamp specifications

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

Aurora A19 produces 740 lumens at full brightness. Its adjustable color temperature ranges from 2200 K to 5000 K.

Aurora requires an always-on wall switch. In-wall dimmer circuits can cause flicker even at the dimmer maximum.

For a factory reset, cycle power five times at one-second intervals. The lamp flashes cyan when reset is complete.

The night preset is fixed at 5 percent brightness and 2200 K. A custom scene can use other settings without changing the preset.
