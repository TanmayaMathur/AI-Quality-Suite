# Scene scheduling and offline behavior

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

Schedules run in the bridge's configured time zone. Changing a phone time zone does not alter existing schedule times.

If two schedules target the same device at the same minute, the schedule edited most recently wins.

Manual overrides pause schedule commands for 45 minutes. Ending the override early in the app restores normal schedule processing.

A scene with an unavailable device continues for reachable devices. The log records the skipped device without retrying it.
