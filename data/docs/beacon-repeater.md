# Beacon Repeater placement

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

Place Beacon Repeater no farther than 9 meters from Harbor Bridge indoors. Dense masonry may require a shorter distance.

Beacon Repeater requires constant USB-C power. It has no battery backup or sensor inputs.

A steady purple light means the repeater is paired but its upstream signal is weak. Move it closer to the bridge before retrying discovery.

Each sensor can route through at most one repeater. Chaining two Beacon Repeaters is unsupported.
