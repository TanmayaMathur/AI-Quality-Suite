# Direct-order returns

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

A direct purchase may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Reseller orders follow the reseller's process.

The return authorization must be issued before dispatch. Put its identifier inside the package along with the complete kit.

Refund review starts when the warehouse scans the package. Allow five business days for review; bank settlement time varies.

Original outbound shipping is refundable only if the order arrived defective or incorrect. Missing accessories are reviewed before a refund is approved.
