# Support intake and account handover

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

A support ticket should contain the product name, firmware version, and status-light pattern. Do not attach access tokens or private contact lists.

Account handover requires the current owner to revoke shared access before pairing the device to the new owner account.

During support escalation, export only the last 24 hours of diagnostics unless a support engineer requests a narrower time range.

The ticket form accepts up to three attachments of 8 MB each. Compress diagnostics locally before submitting oversized files.
