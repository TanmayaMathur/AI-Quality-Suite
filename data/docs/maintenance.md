# Cleaning and storage

Lumenvale Home Systems support handbook. All products and policies are fictional.

Unplug powered devices before cleaning. Use a dry microfiber cloth; sprays and solvent cleaners are prohibited.

Remove the Morrow cell before storage longer than 60 days. Keep the battery separate from metal accessories.

Exercise Stillwater through a full open-close cycle once per quarter if the valve manufacturer permits it.

Before relocating a bridge, export its configuration in the app. The export contains scene definitions but no Wi-Fi credentials.
